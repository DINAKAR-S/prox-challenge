# Page 9 — Controls: Interior Controls

### Figure: Interior Controls (labeled top-down/interior line diagram, cover open)
Line-art view looking into the open top compartment of the welder (case handle visible at top, side carry handles visible left and right), showing the wire spool bay and feed mechanism, with 9 labeled callouts:

- **Cold Wire Feed Switch** — small rectangular switch/button mounted near the top center of the interior, above the feed mechanism.
- **Wire Spool** — large ribbed circular spool holder on the left side of the compartment, labeled "VULCAN" on its center hub.
- **Spool Knob** — the retaining knob at the center of the Wire Spool hub that locks the spool in place.
- **Wire Feed Mechanism** — the drive assembly on the right side of center where the wire spool's wire threads through to the feed rollers (labeled "VULCAN" on its housing).
- **Wire Inlet Liner** — the guide tube/liner running from the wire spool into the Wire Feed Mechanism.
- **Feed Tensioner** — the arm/lever above the Wire Feed Mechanism that applies pressure to the feed rollers, upper-right of the mechanism.
- **Idler Arm** — the pivoting arm to the right of the Feed Tensioner that holds the idler (pressure) roller against the drive roller.
- **Feed Roller Knob** — knob on the Wire Feed Mechanism used to adjust/release roller tension, lower-right of the mechanism.
- **Foot Pedal Socket** — round multi-pin socket at the bottom of the compartment (left of the two bottom sockets), for connecting an optional TIG foot pedal.
- **Wire Feed Control Socket** — round multi-pin socket at the bottom of the compartment (right of the Foot Pedal Socket), for spool-gun wire feed control signal.

Layout notes: Wire Spool + Spool Knob sit on the left; Wire Feed Mechanism (with Feed Tensioner, Idler Arm, Feed Roller Knob) sits center-right; Cold Wire Feed Switch is above the feed mechanism; the two round sockets (Foot Pedal Socket, Wire Feed Control Socket) are at the bottom of the compartment, side by side, Foot Pedal Socket on the left / Wire Feed Control Socket on the right.

This diagram is the key reference for wire-loading questions: wire path runs from the Wire Spool (mounted on the Spool Knob) through the Wire Inlet Liner into the Wire Feed Mechanism, where the Idler Arm/Feed Tensioner hold the wire against the drive roller (adjusted via the Feed Roller Knob) before it exits toward the MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket on the front panel (see page 8).
