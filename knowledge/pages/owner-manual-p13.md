# Page 13 — MIG/Flux-Cored Wire Welding: Gun Cable Connector / DCEN Polarity Setup

13. **Loosen the Knob on the Wire Feed mechanism, then insert the Gun Cable Connector through the hole on the Welder front and into the socket on the Wire Feed.**
14. **Ensure that the Gun Cable Connector is fully inserted into the socket on the Wire Feed mechanism as shown, then tighten the Knob securely.** If Connector is not fully inserted, the gas connection will leak, preventing shielding gas from reaching the welding arc.

**NOTICE:** To prevent damage, do not overtighten the Knob.

15. **Insert the Wire Feed Control Cable through the hole on the Welder front and connect it to the Wire Feed Control Socket inside the machine, then tighten the lock ring on the Cable plug.** Note that the plug on the Cable fits into the Socket in one specific orientation only.

### 16. DCEN — Flux-Cored (Gasless) Polarity Setup
**DCEN Direct Current Electrode Negative Wire Setup for Flux-Cored (gasless) welding:**
Plug Ground Clamp Cable into **Positive (+) Socket**. Plug Wire Feed Power Cable into **Negative (−) Socket**. Twist cables clockwise all the way to lock in place.

### Figure: Gun Cable Connector insertion — Incorrect vs Correct (2 diagrams)
Two labeled close-up drawings of the Wire Feed Mechanism housing (interior), each showing the Gun Cable Connector being pushed into its socket:
- **Top diagram ("Incorrect – Connector not fully inserted"):** Labels "Wire Feed Mechanism" (pointing to the mechanism housing) and "Knob" (pointing to the small hex knob that locks the connector) and "Gun Cable Connector" (pointing to the cable end with a dotted-line gap shown between the connector and the socket, plus a black arrow showing it needs to push further in) — depicts the connector only partially seated, with a visible gap.
- **Bottom diagram ("Correct – Connector fully inserted"):** Same view, but the Wire Feed Control Cable connector is shown fully seated flush against the socket (dotted line shows the fully-inserted path, no gap) — labeled "Wire Feed Control Cable".

### Figure: DCEN Flux-Cored (Gasless) Polarity Setup diagram
Three-quarter view line drawing of the welder with a MIG gun and ground clamp cable attached, captioned "DCEN / Flux-Cored (Gasless) / Polarity Setup". Two leader-line labels:
- **"Wire Feed Power Cable in Negative Socket"** — pointing to the cable plugged into the left/negative bottom-front socket.
- **"Ground Clamp Cable in Positive Socket"** — pointing to the ground clamp's cable plugged into the right/positive bottom-front socket.
This is the opposite wiring from DCEP (see page 14): for flux-cored gasless wire, the electrode (wire feed / gun) side is NEGATIVE and the ground/workpiece side is POSITIVE.
