# Page 12 — MIG/Flux-Cored Wire Welding: Feed Roller Instructions

12. **Feed Roller Instructions:** Check that the Feed Roller is correct for the type of wire being used (solid core or flux-cored) and that it is turned to properly match the wire size marked on the Wire Spool:
    a. **Unscrew the Feed Roller Knob counterclockwise.**
    b. **Remove the Feed Roller Knob to expose the Feed Roller.**
    c. **Flip or replace the Feed Roller as needed and confirm that it is the correct Roller for the type of wire being used and that the number showing is the same as the wire diameter on the Spool.**
    d. **Screw the Feed Roller Knob back into place to secure the Feed Roller.**

### Feed roller groove sizing
| Roller type | Side 1 groove | Side 2 groove |
|---|---|---|
| Solid Core V-Groove | 0.030"/0.035" groove | 0.025" groove |
| Flux-Cored Knurled Groove | 0.045" groove | 0.030"/0.035" groove |

### Figure: Solid Core V-Groove roller cross-section
Side-view diagram of the double-groove feed roller wheel used for solid-core wire. One groove side is labeled "0.030/0.035 groove", the opposite side labeled "0.025 groove". Caption: "Solid Core V-Groove". The roller is reversible/flippable — whichever groove size matches the wire diameter on the spool faces up/engages the wire.

### Figure: Flux-Cored Knurled Groove roller cross-section
Side-view diagram of the double-groove knurled feed roller wheel used for flux-cored wire. One groove side is labeled "0.045 groove", the opposite side labeled "0.030/0.035 groove". Caption: "Flux-Cored Knurled Groove". Knurled (serrated) groove surface (vs. smooth V-groove for solid wire) grips the softer flux-cored wire without crushing it.

Key takeaway: the feed roller is double-sided/reversible — one physical roller wheel serves two wire diameters per wire type (solid-core roller: 0.025" / 0.030"-0.035"; flux-cored roller: 0.030"-0.035" / 0.045"), and it must also be swapped between the Solid Core V-Groove roller and the Flux-Cored Knurled Groove roller depending on which process/wire type is loaded.
