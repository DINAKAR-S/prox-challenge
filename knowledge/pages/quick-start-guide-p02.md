Four cable-setup panels, each paired with a numbered diagram of the welder's front-panel sockets/terminals (numbered circles 1–5 on the diagrams correspond to the numbered steps in the text).

## STICK CABLE SETUP
1. Connect ground clamp to negative terminal.
2. Connect electrode holder to positive terminal.
3. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

### Figure: Stick cable setup diagram
Welder front panel with three connection points called out: ① ground clamp cable → negative terminal (top socket on welder), ③ (unused/disconnected wire-feed power socket, shown routed but not connected), ② electrode holder cable → positive terminal (red-tipped electrode holder plug, bottom socket). Ground clamp (yellow/black clamp) and electrode holder (red handle with clamp jaws holding electrode) are shown at the ends of their respective cables.

## MIG CABLE SETUP
1. Connect ground clamp to negative terminal.
2. Connect wire feed power to positive terminal.
3. Connect MIG gun to wire feed.
4. Connect wire feed control inside welder.

### Figure: MIG cable setup diagram
Welder front panel with four connection points: ④ wire feed control connector (small connector routed to inside the welder, near top), ③ MIG gun connector → wire feed outlet, ① ground clamp cable → negative terminal, ② wire feed power cable → positive terminal. MIG gun (torch with trigger) and ground clamp shown at cable ends.

## FLUX CABLE SETUP
"Follow MIG cable setup but connect ground clamp to positive terminal and wire feed power to negative terminal."
(Polarity is reversed relative to MIG: ground clamp → positive terminal; wire feed power → negative terminal.)

## TIG CABLE SETUP
1. Connect ground clamp to positive terminal.
2. Connect TIG torch to negative terminal.
3. Connect gas line to gas regulator.
4. Connect foot pedal inside welder.
5. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

### Figure: TIG cable setup diagram
Welder front panel with five connection points: ④ foot pedal connector (routed inside welder, top), ③ gas line → gas regulator fitting, ② TIG torch cable → negative terminal, ⑤ gas hose coiled and connected, ① ground clamp cable → positive terminal. Foot pedal (rocker-style pedal with cable), TIG torch (torch with purple/lavender back-cap and long cable), and ground clamp are shown at cable ends.
