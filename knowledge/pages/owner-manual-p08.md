# Page 8 — Controls: Front Panel Controls

### Figure: Front Panel Controls (labeled front-view line diagram of the welder)
Front, straight-on line-art view of the OmniPro 220 case with 13 labeled callouts, leader lines pointing from each label to its part on the machine. Labels and what they point to, top to bottom / left to right:

- **Home Button** — small square button to the left of the LCD display, below the "VULCAN" logo, labeled "HOME" on the unit.
- **Back Button** — small square button to the right of the LCD display, labeled "BACK" on the unit.
- **Control Knob** — the large center rotary dial directly below the LCD display (triangular pointer knob).
- **LCD Display** — the rectangular screen in the center of the upper control panel, between Home and Back buttons.
- **Left Knob** — rotary dial to the left of the Control Knob, marked with "⊕A" (positive/amperage) icon above it.
- **Right Knob** — rotary dial to the right of the Control Knob, marked with "V" (voltage) icon above it.
- **Power Switch** — rocker switch (I/O) located left-of-center on the lower front panel, next to the cooling vents.
- **MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket** — round socket on the lower-left front panel (below the Home Button/Control Knob column), where the MIG gun or spool gun cable connects.
- **Storage Compartment** — recessed compartment behind/above the "VULCAN" nameplate banner, to the right side, above the vents.
- **Spool Gun Gas Outlet** — small round gas fitting at the bottom-left of the panel, left of the Negative Socket.
- **Negative Socket** — the middle-left round threaded socket in the bottom connector row, marked "−" on the unit.
- **Wire Feed Power Cable** — small round socket between the Negative and Positive sockets in the bottom row, marked with a cable-plug icon.
- **Positive Socket** — the rightmost round threaded socket in the bottom connector row, marked "+" on the unit.

Bottom connector row left-to-right order: Spool Gun Gas Outlet → Negative Socket (marked "−") → Wire Feed Power Cable socket → Positive Socket (marked "+").

> **Note:** When using an optional Spool Gun (sold separately), connect the Spool Gun gas hose to the Spool Gun Gas Outlet.

This diagram is the primary reference for "which socket does X plug into" questions for MIG/Flux-Cored setup: the MIG gun (or spool gun) cable plugs into the MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket (upper-left), the work/ground clamp plugs into one of the − or + bottom sockets depending on polarity required by the wire/process being used (see MIG/Flux-Cored Wire Welding and TIG/Stick Welding sections for which polarity goes where), and the Wire Feed Power Cable socket is a separate small socket used for spool-gun wire-feed control signal, distinct from the gas-only Spool Gun Gas Outlet.
