## Assembly Diagram

### Figure: Assembly Diagram (full-page exploded-view illustration)

A full exploded-view technical illustration of the entire welder, showing every part from the Parts List (page 46) pulled apart along its assembly axis, each connected by a leader line to a numbered circle (callout) matching the Part numbers 1–61.

Layout/grouping of callouts as arranged on the page (left to right, top to bottom, matching the exploded 3D positions):

- **Top area — housing shell and handle (numbers 1–7):** 7 (Latch), 6 (Left Cover), 5 (Hinge-left), 4 (Hinge-Right), 3 (Handle Cover), 2 (Handle Base), 1 (Right Cover) — these form the outer case shell and carry handle, shown above the main chassis.
- **Right side — rear panel/cooling stack (numbers 43–61):** 61 (240 VAC Power Cord), 60 (Rear Panel), 59 (Overload Protector), 58 (American Power Socket), 57 (Rear Handle Cover), 56 (Rear Panel), 55 (120 VAC Power Cord), 54 (Y-Type Threaded Tee), 53 (Fan), 52 (IGBT Radiator), 51 (Bridge Rectifiers), 50 (IGBT Radiator), 49 (IGBT Heat Sink Support Bar), 48 (Fast Recovery Diode), 47 (Bridge Rectifier Heatsink), 46 (IGBT), 45 (Transformer), 44 (Fan), 43 (Rectifier Radiator) — shown exploded outward to the right/rear of the chassis, stacked in the order they mount to the rear cooling assembly.
- **Left side — front panel, controls, torch/leads (numbers 8–29):** 16 (Display PCB), 17 (Screen Cover), 18 (Screen Frame), 15 (Protective Cover), 14 (Miller Plug Connector), 13 (Aviation Plug Wire), 12 (Wire Feeder), 11 (Remote Control PCB), 10 (Spool), 9 (PFC Inductance), 8 (Middle Bracket) shown along the internal chassis; 20 (Front Handle Cover), 21 (Front Panel), 22 (Display), 23/24 (Knobs), 25 (Storage Door), 26 (Switch), 27 (American Air Fitting), 28 (Air Connector), 29 (Quick Connector) shown at the front face; 19 (MIG Welding Torch), 30 (Grounding Clamp Assembly), 31 (Welding Clamp Assembly) shown as separate hanging leads/accessories at the lower left.
- **Center-bottom — main chassis/PCB stack (numbers 32–42):** 32 (Polarity Cable), 33 (Output Inductor), 34 (Air Connector), 35 (Solenoid Valve), 36 (Bottom Housing), 37 (Board Insulation Paper), 38 (Main PCB), 39 (Control PCB), 40 (Fast Recovery Diode), 41 (Metal Case Air Way), 42 (Rectifier Radiator Support Bar) — shown mounted inside the central chassis frame beneath the transformer/IGBT stack.

The overall picture depicts the machine "blown apart" along a diagonal axis: outer covers and handle separated upward, front panel/controls/torch/leads separated to the left, and the internal power electronics (transformer, IGBTs, rectifiers, PCBs, fans, rear panel, power cords) separated to the right, all converging on the central bottom-housing/chassis frame. This is a reference diagram only — see the Parts List on page 46 for the full description and quantity of each numbered part.
