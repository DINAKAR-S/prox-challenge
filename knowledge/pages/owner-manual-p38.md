## Stick Weld Diagnosis – Workpiece Heat Control / Weld Penetration

Same three-panel penetration profile diagram style as the wire-weld version (page 35):

| Condition | Description |
|---|---|
| INADEQUATE PENETRATION | Not hot enough — bead sits on surface, visible gap in joint below |
| PROPER PENETRATION | Ideal heat — full triangular fused penetration |
| EXCESS PENETRATION OR BURN-THROUGH | Too hot — deep rounded sagging penetration |

**How to increase workpiece heat and increase penetration (to weld THICKER workpieces properly):**
a. Increase current.
b. Weld more slowly.

**How to reduce workpiece heat and limit penetration (to weld THINNER workpieces properly):**
c. Decrease current.
d. Weld more quickly.

## Example Stick Weld Diagrams

CLEAN WELDS FIRST! Stick welds will have a coat of slag over them until cleaned.

Seven panels, each with a cross-section profile (top) and a real photograph of an actual cleaned stick weld bead (bottom):

### Figure: Good Weld
Photo: straight, uniform, tightly-spaced fish-scale ripple pattern, consistent width. Cross-section: bead sits flush with a modest even crown. Reference weld — no correction needed.

### Figure: Current Too Low
Photo: narrow, ropey, poorly-fused-looking bead with a rough, piled texture. TO CORRECT: Increase the current.

### Figure: Current Too High
Photo: wide, flat, splashy bead with irregular wavy/frayed edges and a burned, "washed out" look. TO CORRECT: Decrease the current.

### Figure: Weld Speed Too Fast
Photo: thin, narrow, widely-spaced ripple bead, understated build-up. TO CORRECT: Weld slower. (Icon: electrode holder angled with arrow labeled traveling along the workpiece.)

### Figure: Weld Speed Too Slow
Photo: wide, heavy bead with tightly bunched ripples and excess pooled material. TO CORRECT: Weld faster. (Icon: electrode holder angled with arrow along the workpiece.)

### Figure: Arc Length Too Short
Photo: narrow bead with small tight ripples, some irregular buildup. TO CORRECT: Increase distance. (Icon: electrode holder held close to and nearly touching the workpiece, arrow pointing up/away indicating "increase distance.")

### Figure: Arc Length Too Long
Photo: wide, rough, spattery bead with an irregular blotchy surface texture. TO CORRECT: Decrease distance. (Icon: electrode holder held at a distance above the workpiece, arrow pointing down toward the workpiece indicating "decrease distance.")
