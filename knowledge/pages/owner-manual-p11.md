# Page 11 — MIG/Flux-Cored Wire Welding: Wire Spool Installation (10-12 lb) / Feed Tensioner

6. **10-12 Pound Wire Spool Installation:** Remove the Wingnut and Spacer. If replacing a Spool, remove the old Spool and all remaining wire from the liners.
7. **Place the Spool Adapter over the Spool Spindle and against the Spool Brake Pad as illustrated.**
8. **Place the new Wire Spool over the Adapter and line up pin on Adapter with hole in Spool.** To prevent wire feed problems, **set the Spool so that it will unwind clockwise.**
9. **Replace the Spacer over the Spool Spindle and secure Spool in place with the Wingnut.**

**Notice:** If Wire Spool can spin freely, Wingnut is too loose. This will cause the welding wire to unravel and unspool which can cause tangling and feeding problems.

10. **Screw the Spool Knob into the Spool Adapter.**
11. **Turn the Feed Tensioner knob counterclockwise to loosen it enough to pull it down to remove tension.** The spring-loaded Idler Arm will move up as shown.

### Figure: Spool Knob / Wingnut / Spacer parts (top of page, isolated part drawings)
Three separate labeled parts drawn top to bottom on the right side of the page: **Spool Knob** (a large hex/fluted knurled knob with a threaded post), **Wingnut** (butterfly/wingnut fastener), **Spacer** (small rectangular sleeve) — each with a leader line to its label. These are the same fasteners referenced for both 1-2 lb and 10-12 lb spool installs, but the 10-12 lb setup additionally uses a Spool Adapter.

### Figure: 10-12 lb Spool Loading (exploded stack diagram)
Vertical exploded-view diagram, top to bottom, showing:
1. **10-12 lb Wire Spool** (large spool, top)
2. **Spool Spindle** (threaded post, labeled pointing to the post under the spool)
3. **10-12 lb Spool Adapter** (a tall cylindrical adapter sleeve the large spool sits on/around — this is the extra part not used in the 1-2 lb install)
4. **Spool Brake Pad** (flat disc beneath the adapter)
5. **Welder Wall** (bottom mounting surface)
Caption: "10-12 lb Spool Loading". Assembly order bottom-to-top: Welder Wall → Spool Brake Pad → Spool Spindle (post, fixed) → 10-12 lb Spool Adapter slides over spindle and sits against brake pad → 10-12 lb Wire Spool slides over the Adapter (pin-to-hole alignment) → Spacer + Wingnut (or Spool Knob per step 10) secure the stack on top.

### Figure: "Wire must unwind in this direction" diagram
Line drawing of the welder viewed from the open interior/top with the wire spool visible in the lower-left of the compartment. A curved dashed arrow circles the spool labeled "Wire must unwind in this direction" — showing the wire paying off the spool in a clockwise direction (viewed from this angle) toward the Wire Feed Mechanism at the right side of the compartment. Confirms the clockwise-unwind instruction from steps 4/8 visually.

- Right panel: **Idler Arm** labeled pointing to the pivoting arm, shown lifted/open (an arrow shows the arm swinging up and to the right) after the tensioner is loosened — exposing the drive roller and groove for threading wire, matching interior-controls layout on page 9 (Feed Tensioner, Idler Arm, Feed Roller Knob).
