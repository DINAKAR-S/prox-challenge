# Page 27 (Item 57812) — TIG/Stick: Stick Setup, Connect Cables / Connect Power Cord

Sidebar tabs (right edge): Safety, Controls, Wire, TIG / STICK (active), Welding Tips, Maintenance.

## Stick Setup

### Connect Cables

1. Plug Ground Clamp Cable into Negative Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.
2. Plug Electrode Holder Cable into Positive Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.

### Figure: Stick cable connection diagram (top)
Isometric view of the Welder (VULCAN logo, LCD control panel and knobs on top-front) with two cables running from the front-lower sockets to handheld tools at bottom-left:
- "Ground Clamp Cable in Negative Socket" — leader line to the left front-panel socket, cable running down to an alligator-style "Ground Clamp" at bottom-left.
- "Electrode Holder Cable in Positive Socket" — leader line to the right front-panel socket, cable running down to a pistol-grip style "Electrode Holder" (stick electrode clamp/holder) at bottom-center.

This establishes Stick polarity wiring on this machine: Ground Clamp → Negative (–) Socket, Electrode Holder → Positive (+) Socket (i.e., DCEP/reverse polarity arrangement for Stick welding).

## Connect Power Cord

Plug either 120 VAC or 240 VAC Power Cord into Power Input Socket.

**Note:** Plug will only fit one way.

### WARNING! TO PREVENT SERIOUS INJURY FROM ACCIDENTAL OPERATION:
> Do not plug cord into wall outlet at this time.
